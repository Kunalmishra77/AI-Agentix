## Section: Product philosophy (Six principles)
- Eyebrow: Product philosophy
- Heading: Six principles that shape every decision.

### Principles (from data)
- 01 No more stacks: Every team is running 15 tools that don't talk to each other. Agentix replaces the sprawl with one operating layer.
- 02 Intent before interface: Teams shouldn't configure AI. They should state a goal and watch the system route it to the right tools, workflows, and humans.
- 03 Human handoff is a feature: AI handles what it can. Risk, compliance, escalation, and judgment calls route to a person — always with full context.
- 04 Output over activity: We measure what gets produced and reviewed — not what was started. Outputs are the unit of progress.
- 05 Governed, not locked: You own your data, your workflows, and your handoff rules. Agentix is infrastructure, not a walled garden.
- 06 One layer, not one app: Agentix isn't a chat tool or a generator. It's the connective tissue between your goals, tools, people, and systems.
